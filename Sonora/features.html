THOUGHTFUL BY DESIGN

A little less noise
around your music.

Sonora brings a music player and downloader into one desktop app. Here’s a closer look at the experience being designed for Windows and Linux.

01 / YOUR COLLECTION

Everything you listen to,
in one clear library.

A calm home for your collection. The Sonora preview groups music into familiar views such as albums, recently added, and playlists, so it’s easier to find the right thing without digging through menus.

See the player preview ↗

02 / YOUR DOWNLOADS

A downloader,
alongside your player.

Listening and downloading belong in the same straightforward desktop experience. Sonora is being made as a player and downloader; supported music sources and download formats have not yet been announced.

Read the download FAQ ↗

03 / YOUR LISTENING

Playback stays
out of the way.

Artwork, track details, and playback controls have their own clear place in the preview, leaving the rest of your library easy to browse. Familiar details, without a busy screen competing for attention.
