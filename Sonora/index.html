GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers.

Which platforms are planned?

Sonora is being prepared for Windows and Linux. Visit each platform page for system details and the current release status.

Can I download Sonora today?

Yes. Beta ZIP downloads are available for Windows and Linux on their platform pages. Official installers and distribution packages are still in preparation.

Where will the downloader get music?

Supported sources have not been announced. We’ll list them once they’re confirmed, rather than imply compatibility that isn’t documented.

Is the player preview a live app?

No. It’s a visual preview of the Sonora desktop experience, not a working player or installer.
